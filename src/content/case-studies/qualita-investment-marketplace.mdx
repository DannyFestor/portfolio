## Situation

<Aside>it worked, until it got slow</Aside>

A customer of Qualita ran a platform where people register the items they hold as investments, buy and sell them on a marketplace and follow how their prices change. Three applications stood on one backend: the user web app, the admin app and the public service website.

The backend had grown with the marketplace, card payments, contracts and monthly reports. Some of its queries had become slow; one took seconds to answer.

## Decisions

<Aside>measure first</Aside>

**Measure before changing anything.** I ran the slow queries through EXPLAIN ANALYZE, so the database itself showed where the time went, instead of my guesses.

**Fix the cause with the smallest change.** For the worst query, that change was one index.

**Keep the model honest.** Whenever the data model changed, I changed the migrations, the seed data and the diagrams with it.

**Delete what nobody calls.** From late 2024 I proposed improvements and a clean-up of the backend and carried them out, removing GraphQL queries, mutations and types that nothing used any more.

## Result

<Aside>one index</Aside>

The query that took seconds answers in milliseconds. The fix shipped as a database performance hotfix in May 2025, and the API came out of the clean-up smaller than it went in.
